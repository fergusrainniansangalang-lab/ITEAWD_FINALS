# ITEAWD-Finals: RaiJouKaKu (ライジョウカク) Web Application

**RaiJouKaKu** is an authentic Japanese lifestyle & retail e-commerce web platform engineered for the **ITEAWD Finals Examination**. It provides a unified digital storefront spanning 7 distinct departments with 35 authentic Japanese products.

## 🏪 Store Concept & Categorization (35 Products)
RaiJouKaKu features 5 authentic Japanese products in each of the 7 required verticals:
1. **Clothing (衣類)**: Haori kimonos, Kojima raw selvedge denim, silk yukatas, Harajuku hoodies, Jinbei sets.
2. **Grocery / Foods (食品)**: Sapporo premium draft packs, Kyoto Uji matcha, Tonkotsu ramen kits, Tamari soy sauce, Hokkaido white chocolate.
3. **Electronics (電子機器)**: Sony WH-1000XM5 Tokyo edition, HHKB mechanical keyboards, Audio-Technica turntables, Zojirushi rice cookers, Fujifilm Instax cameras.
4. **Shoes (靴)**: Asics Gel-Kayano 14 Japanese edition, Mizuno Wave Rider 27, Hinoki geta clogs, Onitsuka Tiger Mexico 66 Nippon Made, Tabi ninja boots.
5. **Cleaning Supplies / Home (清掃用品)**: Muji aroma diffusers, Shuro palm brooms, Binchotan charcoal purifiers, Imabari towels, Wabi-sabi incense burners.
6. **Sports Equipment (スポーツ用品)**: Molten FIBA official basketballs, Mizuno pro maple bats, Shureido kendo shinai swords, Manduka cherry blossom mats, Nanbu Tekki kettlebells.
7. **Books (書籍)**: Book of Five Rings, Japanese Woodworking Tools, Wabi-Sabi philosophy, Tokyo architectural guides, Genki textbook sets.

## 🌸 Secret Admin Console
To access the administrative console:
1. Navigate to `/authentication/login` (or `/login`).
2. Click the **Sakura Flower Icon** at the top of the login card **5 times**.
3. In the security dialog, enter the administrator passkey: `JAPANISLIFE`.
4. The **RaiJouKaKu Master Administrator Console** will open, providing controls for inventory status toggling, shipment dispatching, revenue KPI tracking, and customer service ticket management.
